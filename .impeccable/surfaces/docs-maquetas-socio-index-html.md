# Surface brief: vista del socio (preview)

Scope: socio-facing app screens of Reto Fit CCA (Inicio, Registrar sesión, Técnica, Progreso y ranking), delivered first as a preview at docs/maquetas/socio/index.html before porting into the app. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: socio between sets or at the club, phone in one hand; knows what to train today, logs sets, checks technique, sees own progress. Constraints: socio sees only their own place and points (no other names); body fat is an estimate; sample data must be labeled; tabs stay Inicio, Rutina, Progreso, Nutrición, Perfil.

Memorable moment: the scorecard strip, 12 weeks scored like holes against par.

Unresolved: whether nutrition stays; real technique videos (photos for now).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The reto is the club's scorecard. Twelve weeks read as twelve holes, each week's sessions scored against its meta like strokes against par. Refuses the dark ground with neon accent and progress rings that every fitness app ships, including this app's own previous look.

OWN-WORLD: White card stock on a cool fairway-tinted ground; a deep club-blue shell band carrying the C logo; fairway green and logo lime for met goals; navy ink; hairline blue-grey scorecard rules; amber only for pending evaluation, red only for pain or risk. Barlow Condensed numerals and headings, Barlow for text, tabular figures everywhere numbers live.

STORY: The socio sees where they are in the 12 weeks, what their trainer planned for today, starts it in one tap, logs each set with big controls, checks technique without leaving, and sees their own change and place.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Inicio at 390 px: blue band (logo, Semana 5 de 12, phase, day 33/84); the 12-column scorecard strip directly under it, current week column in blue; "Hoy te toca" card with day name, exercise count, duration and trainer; full-width primary button at thumb height; tab bar.
